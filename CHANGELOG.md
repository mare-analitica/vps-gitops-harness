## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-15

### Added

- `vps-gitops` AI skill: access levels and guardrails, reference architecture,
  deploy queue, secrets, CI pipeline, team access and capacity references.
- Troubleshooting catalog generalized from production incidents.
- `diagnose.sh` read-only server report and `jenkins.sh` REST CLI.
- CI quality gates: shellcheck, markdownlint, actionlint and full-history
  gitleaks, with actions pinned by SHA and images by digest.
- Community health files, issue forms and pull request template.
- Apache-2.0 license and NOTICE.
